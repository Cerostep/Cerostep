# DevOps × AI Agents: Learning, Practice, and Experience Sharing

This is a bilingual blog documenting technical learning and engineering practice at the intersection of DevOps and AI Agents. It explores how to build, deliver, operate, and continuously improve AI applications. The content draws on ongoing learning, hands-on experimentation, and lessons learned, with an emphasis on translating concepts into clear design principles, reusable practices, and real-world problem-solving insights.

## Topics

- **DevOps and Platform Engineering**: Automation, CI/CD, cloud-native technologies, observability, reliability, and developer experience.
- **AI Agent Engineering**: Retrieval and tool use, agent orchestration, context management, evaluation, and safety guardrails.
- **AI Application Delivery and Operations**: Integrating AI capabilities into existing systems while continuously measuring and improving quality, cost, latency, and reliability.
- **Learning Notes and Engineering Insights**: Documenting practical experiments, technical trade-offs, common challenges, and reusable lessons learned.
