# About Cerostep

Cerostep is a blog about learning and engineering at the intersection of DevOps and AI agents. It explores how to build, deliver, operate, and continuously improve AI-powered applications.

The posts bring together ongoing study, hands-on experiments, and practical lessons. Topics include:

- DevOps, platform engineering, automation, and observability.
- AI agent workflows, retrieval, tool use, orchestration, and evaluation.
- Operating AI applications with attention to quality, cost, latency, and reliability.
- Technical decisions, experiments, troubleshooting, and reusable lessons.

The goal is to share practical, verifiable engineering experience—not simply to use more models or agents.
